# Hi, I'm Léo 👋

Welcome to my GitHub. I'm Léo, a Master in Finance student at Bocconi and an ESSEC alumnus, passionate about financial markets and the many roles around them: structuring, trading, sales and hedge funds.

On this page I share personal and academic projects: research, strategy backtests, derivatives pricing and market analysis. Most of them are in Python, some in Excel/VBA, and many use real market data (Bloomberg, FRED, ECB).

I will publish a new project every week.

---

## 1. Investment Banking

### 1.1 Structuring
- [Worst-of Phoenix Autocall](https://github.com/LeoYakass15/autocall_phoenix): local vol Monte Carlo pricer of a Barclays note, with Greeks and a delta/vega hedge backtest
- [CMS Steepener](https://github.com/LeoYakass15/cms_steepener): Goldman Sachs callable CMS steepener priced with SABR and G2++, issuer call and fair leverage
  

### 1.2 Trading
- [Monte Carlo & Delta Hedging](https://github.com/LeoYakass15/monte_carlo_delta_hedging): Monte Carlo pricer with variance reduction and discrete delta hedging P&L

  
### 1.3 Sales
- [FX Hedging Tool](https://github.com/LeoYakass15/fx_hedging_tool): forward, option, collar and TARF hedges for a corporate exporter (Excel/VBA)
- [IR Hedging Tool](https://github.com/LeoYakass15/ir_hedging_tool): €STR/Euribor curves, swap, cap and collar hedges for a corporate loan (Excel/VBA)
- [Commodities Hedging Tool](https://github.com/LeoYakass15/commo_hedging_tool): jet fuel / Brent hedges for an airline (Excel/VBA)
